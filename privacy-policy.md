# Chillax privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Chillax is a media player for TV. It does not include any channels, movies or shows. You connect it to a service you already have, by entering your own playlist address (M3U) or Xtream Codes login.

## What Chillax stores, and where

Everything stays on your TV:

- **Your provider details** (server address, username, password or playlist address) and any API keys you enter. These are stored encrypted on the device.
- **Watch progress, favorites and subtitle preferences**, so you can resume where you left off.

Chillax has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer receives none of this information.

## Who Chillax connects to

- **Your provider**, using the details you entered, to load your playlist and play streams.
- **The Movie Database (TMDB)**, only if you add a TMDB API key: the titles you browse are sent to TMDB to fetch artwork, descriptions, ratings and cast. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
- **OpenSubtitles and Gestdown**, only when you search for subtitles: the title being played is sent to find matching subtitles.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

Clear Chillax's storage in your TV's settings (Settings, Apps, Chillax, Clear data) or uninstall the app. That removes everything listed above.

## Children

Chillax is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
